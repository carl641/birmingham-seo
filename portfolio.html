Local Service Business | 12-Month Campaign

We rebuilt their local SEO from the ground up — optimizing their Google Business Profile, building consistent citations across Birmingham directories, and creating service-area pages for each city they cover.

+245% Organic Traffic +180% Leads Top 3 Rankings

Key Results:

  • Showing in Google Local Pack for 15+ service keywords

  • ROI of 420% in the first year
