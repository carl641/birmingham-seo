Local SEO for Birmingham

Local SEO is the foundation of visibility for Birmingham businesses that serve a specific geographic area. We optimize your presence so you show up when customers in your part of the metro search for your services.

  • Google Business Profile optimization and management
  • Local citation building (Birmingham BBB, Chamber of Commerce, Yelp, industry directories)
  • Local keyword targeting by neighborhood and city
  • Review generation and reputation management
  • Local link building through Birmingham-area publications and organizations

Technical SEO

The technical foundation of your website determines how well Google can crawl, index, and rank your pages. We audit and fix the issues that hold Birmingham businesses back in search results.

  • Comprehensive site audits with prioritized action items
  • Page speed optimization (Core Web Vitals)
  • Mobile-first optimization
  • Schema markup and structured data implementation
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration
  • Crawl error resolution and site architecture improvements

Keyword Research & Strategy

We identify the search terms that Birmingham customers actually use when looking for businesses like yours, then build a strategy to rank for the ones that drive the most valuable traffic.

  • Birmingham-specific keyword analysis and search volume data
  • Competitor keyword gap analysis
  • Search intent mapping for local queries
  • Long-tail keyword identification for niche Birmingham markets
  • Keyword difficulty assessment and prioritization

Content Marketing

Content is what Google ranks. We create SEO-optimized content that targets the keywords your Birmingham customers are searching for while providing genuine value that builds trust and drives conversions.

  • Content strategy aligned with Birmingham market opportunities
  • Blog posts, service pages, and location pages
  • Landing page optimization for local conversions
  • Content gap analysis against Birmingham competitors
  • Existing content refresh and optimization

Link Building & Digital PR

Backlinks from relevant, authoritative websites signal to Google that your business is trustworthy. We earn links through relationships, quality content, and outreach to Birmingham-area publications and industry sites.
